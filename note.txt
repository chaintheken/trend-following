What we're specifically building
Spot Bitcoin (and eventually a few other liquid coins — ETH, SOL maybe) on hourly or daily timeframes, using Binance data via ccxt. Paper-traded, not real money. Backtested honestly with walk-forward validation. Metrics computed from scratch so you understand what they mean.
Timeframe choice matters: we're doing hourly/daily, not minute or tick data. Reasons — higher timeframes are more forgiving of infrastructure weakness, fees eat you alive on low timeframes, and strategies on hourly/daily timescales are where your math and reasoning matter more than your latency. High-frequency is a different sport you don't want to play as a solo beginner.

What we are not doing
Not touching leverage or futures yet. Spot only. Leverage adds complexity (funding rates, liquidation risk) that'll distract from the core learning.
Not touching DeFi/on-chain trading yet. That's a valid extension for later. For now, centralized exchange data because it's clean and easy.
Not using real money for at least 6 weeks. This is non-negotiable. The goal is building an honest backtest and paper-trading system. Real money before you trust your backtest is how people blow up accounts.
Not trying to find a profitable strategy. We're trying to build the measurement system that would tell you if a strategy is profitable. This is the subtle part most beginners skip.
